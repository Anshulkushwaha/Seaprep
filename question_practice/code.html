Describe the lights and shapes required for a vessel constrained by her draft according to COLREGs Rule 28.

business_center Frequently asked by Mediterranean Shipping Company (MSC)

Suggested Answer Structure

According to Rule 28 of the International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea (COLREGs):

  • Underway (Lights): In addition to the lights prescribed for a power-driven vessel (masthead lights, sidelights, and sternlight), she may exhibit where they can best be seen three all-round red lights in a vertical line.
  • Day shape: A cylinder where it can best be seen.
  • Note: The term "constrained by her draft" applies only to power-driven vessels. The definition is found in Rule 3(h).

Interviewer's Focus

They are looking for precision. Mentioning that it only applies to power-driven vessels shows a deep understanding of the definitions in Rule 3, not just rote memorization of Rule 28.
